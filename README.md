## THE MISSION

**Crystal OSINT Hub** showcases my independent online-safety research, focused primarily on social VR spaces such as **VRChat**. I look for concerning behavior and statements that may indicate exploitation or predatory conduct, and work to preserve relevant information responsibly.

My approach starts with **observation and listening**. Conversations can surface concerning claims or admissions, but **a statement alone does not establish that a crime occurred**. Any concerns need context, corroboration, and careful documentation—not public accusations.

This is a portfolio of my described approach, not a claim of law-enforcement authority or verified outcomes.

## HOW I WORK

**01 — RECON / OBSERVATION**  
I spend time in social VR environments observing interactions and listening for concerning behavior. The starting point is understanding context, not assuming guilt.

**02 — CONVERSATION & CONTEXT**  
When people voluntarily share statements or public-facing social account information, I assess relevant context and avoid assuming that a username or unverified claim establishes someone's identity.

**03 — REVIEW & DOCUMENTATION**  
I keep records of relevant observations and, where lawful and appropriate, recordings or other supporting material. I organize suspected incidents in separate private evidence logs so details are not mixed across cases.

**04 — VERIFY & REPORT**  
I distinguish direct observations, reported statements, and independent corroboration. Serious concerns belong with appropriate platform safety teams or authorities—not in a public callout or doxxing post.

## AREAS OF FOCUS

| Capability | Scope |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Social VR observation** | Primarily VRChat; tracking context and concerning behavior in social spaces |
| **Open-source research** | Relevant publicly available pages, posts, and social information |
| **Statement review** | Accurately recording what was said without equating claims with proof |
| **Source verification** | Cross-checking timelines and identifying uncertainty or mistaken identity |

| **Responsible reporting** | Clear, factual summaries for appropriate reporting channels |

## PRIVATE BY DESIGN

**My research tools are not being released.** This public repository contains only the portfolio website and its documentation. It does **not** contain investigative recordings, case files, victim information, suspect profiles, internal tool source code, Discord RPC, VRChat OSC integrations, or access to private storage.

Public accusations can harm people and compromise legitimate reporting. Nothing here identifies or declares anyone guilty.
