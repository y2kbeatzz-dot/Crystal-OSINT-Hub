About me:

Welcome to my corner of the old internet! I'm Crystal, an independent OSINT researcher focused on online safety, responsible public-source research, evidence organization, and documentation involving online communities including Discord and VRChat.

A lot of my attention goes to Discord and VRChat. I care about what happens behind accounts and avatars, especially when harmful behavior is dismissed, hidden, or difficult to report. ✧ my story // behind the avatars _ □ ×

[ PERSONAL ACCOUNT ] [ DISCORD + VRCHAT ] [ PUBLIC-SOURCE RESEARCH ]

Why I started paying attention

I’m Crystal. My OSINT work comes from seeing concerning behavior in online spaces and wanting those reports to be taken seriously. VRChat is a major focus for me, alongside Discord and the wider social connections around them. Real people are behind the avatars, and what happens in those spaces matters.

What I actually do

I observe public and social spaces, pay attention to concerning statements and patterns, review publicly available context where appropriate, and organize what I can substantiate. I keep notes and supporting material privately rather than turning someone’s name or an unverified allegation into public entertainment.

This work is about research, documentation, verification, and responsible reporting—not harassment, doxxing, exposing private case files, or declaring people guilty.

✉ reporting reality // the part people don't see _ □ ×

[ 100+ REPORTS SUBMITTED ] [ PERSONAL EXPERIENCE ]

Reporting isn't always straightforward

Reporting can be frustrating; in my experience, online reports haven’t always got attention, so I’ve sometimes followed up in person.

Putting together a responsible report takes time: keeping the context straight, distinguishing what was actually observed from what is only suspected, and finding the appropriate place to submit it. Sometimes you never get a detailed response or learn what happened afterward.

What the number means

I’ve submitted over 100 reports concerning suspected misconduct in Discord and VRChat-related spaces. That is a count of reports I say I submitted—not a count of confirmed offenders, arrests, convictions, substantiated cases, or platform enforcement actions. Some concerns may require follow-up, and I don't claim to know every outcome.

01. OBSERVE02. DOCUMENT03. VERIFY04. REPORT05. FOLLOW UP

If you encounter concerning conduct: prioritize safety, preserve lawful evidence without sharing it publicly, use official reporting tools, and contact appropriate authorities if someone is in immediate danger. Don't conduct vigilante stings or take over an investigation.
