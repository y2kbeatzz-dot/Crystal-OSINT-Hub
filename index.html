01 — MISSION

I start by listening.
Then I check the facts.

My research begins with observation in social VR environments, especially VRChat. I listen to conversations, assess concerning statements in context, and review voluntarily shared or publicly available information. Statements and allegations are not proof of wrongdoing.

Private case materials, subject identities, internal techniques, and research records are intentionally excluded from this public website.

05 — ONLINE SAFETY AWARENESS
THE ISSUE IS REAL

Behind the screen,
real people are at risk.

Online grooming, coercion, exploitation, and sextortion can happen in social spaces, including games and virtual worlds. Warning signs deserve attention—but identifying them is not the same as proving an allegation. Protect young people, document concerns responsibly, and use official reporting channels.

07 — FIRSTHAND REPORTING EXPERIENCE

Reporting matters.
Follow-up matters too.

Reporting suspected online predators can be frustrating. In my personal experience, online reports haven't always received the attention they deserve, and I've sometimes needed to follow up in person. If you're reporting suspected exploitation, document your concerns, use official reporting channels, and follow up when necessary.

This describes a personal experience, not a universal requirement to report in person. Official online reporting options remain available.
